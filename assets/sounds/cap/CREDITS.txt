The files are copied unmodified, keeping their original paths inside "sound/".
They may only be used non-commercially, and redistributions must keep this license.

Changes: the larger files (gate_roll_long, dhd_usual_dial, hyperwindow, missilefire,
asgard_beam, atlantis_door_open) were converted from WAV to MP3 (128 kbps) to save space;
the audio itself is otherwise unmodified. All files are packed into ../cap-sounds.js by
tools/pack-sounds.js so the game can load them without fetching.
